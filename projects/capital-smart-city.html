Everything in place, from day one.

Capital Smart City is a DTCP-approved layout of three blocks (A, B and C) at Kotra X Roads near Kalwakurthy, announced by a grand entrance arch. It sits in reach of the Regional Ring Road, NH167K and NH765/167, and is roughly five minutes from a planned 412-acre food processing unit — a corridor where infrastructure is arriving ahead of speculation.

  • 33' CC Roads
  • Sports & Play Areas
  • Electrical Lines with Street Lights

  • Well-Designed Parks/Gardens
  • Gated Compound Wall
