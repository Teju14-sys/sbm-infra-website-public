ABOUT CAPITAL SMART CITY
What is Capital Smart City and where is it located?

Capital Smart City is a DTCP-approved residential open-plot layout by SBM Infra Projects, located at Kotra X Roads & Kalwakurthy. Capital Smart City is a DTCP-approved layout of three blocks (A, B and C) at Kotra X Roads near Kalwakurthy, announced by a grand entrance arch. It sits in reach of the Regional Ring Road, NH167K and NH765/167, and is roughly five minutes from a planned 412-acre food processing unit — a corridor where infrastructure is arriving ahead of speculation.

What approvals does Capital Smart City have?

Capital Smart City is DTCP approved with 100% clear title. Layout permission: LP 62·65·68/2022. RERA registration: D.T.L.P Nos. 62/2022, 65/2022, 68/2022 /DTCPO/NGKL (Blocks A/B/C). All reference numbers are shown as filed.

What amenities are included in Capital Smart City?

Capital Smart City includes Grand Entrance Arch, 33' CC Roads, Sports & Play Areas, Electrical Lines with Street Lights, Underground Drainage & Septic Tank, Every-Plot Tap Connection, and more. All development is completed before plots are handed over.
