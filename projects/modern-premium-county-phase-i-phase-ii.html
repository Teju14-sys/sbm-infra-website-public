Everything in place, from day one.

Modern Premium County by SBM Infra India Pvt. Ltd. is a DTCP-approved gated residential villa plot development of 20.88 acres across two phases at Thandra Village, Kalwakurthy Mandal, Nagarkurnool District. The layout holds 299 plots in all — 128 in Phase I and 171 in Phase II — laid out along 33', 40' and proposed 40' wide internal CC roads, with roughly 10% of each phase reserved as open space for parks. It sits on the Thandra Village 4-line wide road just off the existing 100' wide road between Hyderabad and Srisailam, near the Regional Ring Road, about 45 minutes from RGIA and a 30-minute drive from the 30,000-acre Bharat Future City.
