ABOUT PRAKRUTHI HILLS
What is Prakruthi Hills and where is it located?

Prakruthi Hills is a DTCP-approved residential open-plot layout by SBM Infra Projects, located at Srisailam X Road, Kalwakurthy. Prakruthi Hills is a gated layout of open residential plots at Srisailam X Road, Kalwakurthy — surveyed in Kondareddipalle, Vangoor Mandal, Nagarkurnool District. The community is developed with 40' and 33' CC roads, fruit and avenue plantation, designed parks and an overhead water tank. The layout is now fully booked.

What approvals does Prakruthi Hills have?

Prakruthi Hills is DTCP approved with 100% clear title. Layout permission: TLP 72/2023/H. RERA registration: T.L.P No. 72/2023/H · TS RERA P01600008503. All reference numbers are shown as filed.

What amenities are included in Prakruthi Hills?

Prakruthi Hills includes 40' & 33' CC Roads, Sports & Play Areas, Fruit & Avenue Plantation, Underground Drainage & Septic Tank, Overhead Water Tank, Well-Designed Parks/Gardens, and more. All development is completed before plots are handed over.

How do I get directions to Prakruthi Hills?

You can get directions to Prakruthi Hills via Google Maps using the coordinates 16.6569182,78.5763131, or use the Get Directions button on this page.

Is Prakruthi Hills still available?

Prakruthi Hills is fully booked. Resale opportunities do come up from time to time through SBM's lifetime maintenance and resale service. Contact the team to register interest or ask about current resale listings.
