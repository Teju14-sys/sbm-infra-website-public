Developed Before You Buy

Everything in place, from day one.

Green Meadows sits inside Kalwakurthy Municipality, adjacent to the Municipality Park and the Gurukula Residential School, with quick reach to NH-44 at Jadcherla and NH-167 towards Vijayawada. The layout is delivered in two approved phases (PASHE I & II), with 60' to 30' BT roads, tiled pathways, drip-irrigated avenue plantation and a gated compound wall.

  • 60'/40'/33'/30' BT Roads
  • Sports & Play Areas
  • Pathways with Tiles
  • Street Lights & Transformer
  • Avenue Plantation with Drip System
  • Underground Drainage & Septic Tank
  • Overhead Water Tank
  • Gated Compound Wall
  • Underground Electricity (Premium)
