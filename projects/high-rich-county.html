ABOUT HIGH RICH COUNTY
What is High Rich County and where is it located?

High Rich County is a DTCP-approved residential open-plot layout by SBM Infra Projects, located at JP Nagar Junction. High Rich County offers open residential plots in three approved parts (A, B and C) at JP Nagar Junction, a growing crossing on the Hyderabad–Srisailam corridor close to NH-167 and NH-167K. The gated layout is developed with 33' CC roads, designed parks, underground drainage and a tap connection to every plot.

What approvals does High Rich County have?

High Rich County is DTCP approved with 100% clear title. Layout permission: LP 10·13·15/2024. RERA registration: Part A/B/C L.P Nos. 10/2024, 13/2024, 15/2024 DTCPO/NGKL · TS RERA P01600009169 / P01600009152 / P01600009158. All reference numbers are shown as filed.

What amenities are included in High Rich County?

High Rich County includes 33' CC Roads, Sports & Play Areas, Underground Drainage & Septic Tank, Every-Plot Tap Connection, Well-Designed Parks/Gardens, Gated Compound Wall, and more. All development is completed before plots are handed over.
