Developed Before You Buy

Everything in place, from day one.

High Rich County offers open residential plots in three approved parts (A, B and C) at JP Nagar Junction, a growing crossing on the Hyderabad–Srisailam corridor close to NH-167 and NH-167K. The gated layout is developed with 33' CC roads, designed parks, underground drainage and a tap connection to every plot.

  • 33' CC Roads
  • Sports & Play Areas
  • Underground Drainage & Septic Tank
  • Every-Plot Tap Connection
  • Well-Designed Parks/Gardens
  • Gated Compound Wall
  • Electrical Lines with Transformer
  • Underground Electricity (Premium)
